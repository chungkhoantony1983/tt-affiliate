Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

1. Introduction

TT Content Publisher ("the App") is a content preparation tool that uploads videos created by the account owner to their TikTok inbox as drafts. This privacy policy explains how data is handled by the App.

2. Data Collection

The App only processes data of the TikTok account that explicitly authorizes it via Login with TikTok. No other user's data or third-party personal data is collected.

The only data processed by the App:

Data TypePurposeStorage
TikTok basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar)Show which TikTok account is connectedLocal database
TikTok OAuth tokensAuthenticate with TikTok APILocal encrypted storage
Video content metadataTrack which videos were sent to the TikTok inboxLocal database

3. Data Sharing

The App does not share any data with third parties. All data remains on the account owner's local device.

4. Third-Party Services

The App interacts with the following services solely for content uploading:

  • TikTok API — to upload videos as drafts to the user's own TikTok inbox. The user reviews, edits and posts each video in the TikTok app.

These interactions are governed by TikTok's own privacy policy and terms.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored locally on the user's device
  • OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest
  • No cloud storage or external servers are used
  • No data is transmitted to the App developer

6. User Rights

The account owner has full control over all data and may delete it at any time. Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok app settings (Security → Manage app permissions), after which stored tokens become invalid and are deleted.

7. Children's Privacy

This App is not intended for use by children under 13. No data from minors is collected.

8. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated at any time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please open an issue at github.com/chungkhoantony1983/tt-affiliate/issues.
