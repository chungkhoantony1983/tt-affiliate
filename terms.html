1. Overview

TT Content Publisher ("the App") is a content preparation tool that helps a creator produce short product-review videos and upload them to their own TikTok inbox as drafts.

2. Use of Service

The user connects their TikTok account via Login with TikTok. The App uses TikTok's Content Posting API (Upload) to send rendered videos to the user's TikTok inbox. The App never posts content automatically: the user opens each draft in the TikTok app, adds the caption, chooses privacy settings, discloses commercial content where applicable, and decides whether to post it.

3. Content Guidelines

All content uploaded through the App:

  • Is original content created by the account owner
  • Complies with TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service
  • Does not infringe on third-party intellectual property rights
  • Does not contain harmful, misleading, or prohibited content

4. Data Handling

The App processes only the data of the TikTok account that authorized it. No third-party user data is collected, stored, or processed. See our Privacy Policy for details.

5. API Usage

The App uses TikTok's APIs in accordance with TikTok's Developer Terms of Service and API usage guidelines. All API interactions are limited to the authorized account, and access can be revoked at any time from TikTok app settings.
