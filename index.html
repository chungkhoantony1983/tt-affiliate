TT Content Publisher

TT Content Publisher helps a creator prepare short product-review videos and send them to their own TikTok inbox as drafts.

How it works

  1. Connect your TikTok account with Login with TikTok.
  2. The App renders a short video from your product information and script.
  3. The App uploads the video to your TikTok inbox using the Content Posting API (Upload).
  4. You receive a TikTok notification, open the draft in the TikTok app, add the caption, choose privacy settings and post it yourself.

The App never posts to TikTok on your behalf. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok app settings.

Permissions requested

  • user.info.basic — show which TikTok account is connected.
  • video.upload — upload videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts.
